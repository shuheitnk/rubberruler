# Design Documents of rubberruler

The documents for building rubberruler. Claude Code starts from `CLAUDE.md` at the root of the repository.

| Document | Content | Readers |
|---|---|---|
| [spec.md](spec.md) | What is built. The single source of truth for the specification (with requirement IDs) | Everyone; read first |
| [implementation.md](implementation.md) | The order of construction (M0 to M9), verified facts and pitfalls, tests, and tools | Implementers |
| [decisions.md](decisions.md) | The decisions and their reasons (D1 to D44). Not reconsidered | When in doubt |
| [ux-design.md](ux-design.md) | The grounds of the rules on the user experience (human factors and software engineering), and the plan of evaluation | Readers who want the reasons of the design |

Where the documents disagree, `spec.md` is correct. A change of the specification is made in `spec.md` first.

The documents are in English since 2026-10-14. They describe the repository as of 0.1.0; earlier states are in the history before 0.1.0.
